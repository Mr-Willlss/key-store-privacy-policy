Keystore

Privacy Policy

Keystore is a privacy-first Android clipboard manager and optional keyboard. This policy explains what data the app handles, how it is stored, and what happens when you use features such as clipboard history, writing capture, and voice typing.

Last updated August 14, 2026
Applies to Keystore and Keystore Keyboard

Data Keystore handles

Keystore stores text that you intentionally copy while the Keystore app or Keystore Keyboard can access the Android clipboard. You may also edit and format individual saved entries.

Saved clipboard content is encrypted in the app's private local database using a key protected by Android Keystore.

Keystore does not upload clipboard entries, edited text, keyboard input, contacts, identifiers, or usage histories to the developer. It does not sell or share user data for advertising.

Voice typing

Voice typing is optional and requires microphone permission. Audio recognition is provided by the speech-recognition service configured on your Android device.

That service may process audio according to its own privacy policy. Keystore receives the recognized text and does not retain microphone recordings.

Internet access

Internet access is used only when you request an optional English dictionary download. Clipboard content is not included in dictionary requests.

Clipboard and keyboard limitations

Android restricts background clipboard access. Keystore reads the clipboard only when Android exposes it while the app or Keystore Keyboard is active.

Keystore excludes password and other sensitive editor fields from keyboard learning and suggestions.

Retention and deletion

Clipboard entries remain on the device until you delete them or uninstall Keystore.

  • You can move individual entries to trash.
  • You can permanently delete entries.
  • You can delete all saved content at once.

Uninstalling the app removes its local database and device-bound encryption key.

Security

Keystore disables Android backup for its application data. Local clipboard content is encrypted before database storage.

No security measure is guaranteed to prevent every possible form of device compromise.

Children

Keystore is not directed specifically to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children on developer-controlled servers.

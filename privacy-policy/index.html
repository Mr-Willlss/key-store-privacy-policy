Privacy Policy

Keystore is an Android clipboard archive and optional keyboard. Clipboard capture and cloud synchronization are controlled separately by you.

Cloud storage: Cloud sync is off until you sign in and turn it on. Cloud content is protected by account access rules and provider encryption at rest, but is not end-to-end encrypted. Do not sync passwords, payment details, recovery codes, or other secrets.

Data Keystore handles

Keystore stores text you intentionally copy while the app or keyboard can access Android's clipboard. Entries can also include titles, timestamps, copy counts, favorites, tags, trash state, and formatting. Keystore does not intentionally save every character you type. Android controls when clipboard text is available, and some apps or devices restrict clipboard reads.

On this device, clipboard content is encrypted in the app's private Room database with AES-GCM using a key protected by Android Keystore. Device encryption keys are not shared between devices.

Optional cloud sync and accounts

Cloud sync is off by default. If you sign in with Google and enable sync, eligible clipboard entries are sent over HTTPS to your account in Keystore Cloud, powered by Supabase. The cloud stores clipboard text, formatting data, and archive metadata so you can access synced entries from the web archive and other signed-in Keystore devices.

Supabase Row Level Security restricts each account to its own rows. The provider encrypts stored data at rest. This is not end-to-end encryption: Keystore Cloud can process readable clipboard text. Entries Keystore identifies as sensitive are excluded from sync. Google sign-in is used for your Keystore account; Keystore does not request access to Gmail, Drive, or other Google services.

Turning sync off stops future synchronization but leaves existing cloud copies in your account. You can delete cloud copies from the app's Cloud sync settings. Your on-device archive remains available when cloud copies are deleted or you sign out.

Voice typing and dictionary

Voice typing is optional and requires microphone permission. Audio recognition is provided by the speech-recognition service configured on your Android device, which may process audio under its own privacy policy. Keystore receives recognized text and does not retain microphone recordings.

The app may download an optional English dictionary pack when you request it. Clipboard text is not included in dictionary requests.

Retention and deletion

Local entries remain until you delete them or uninstall Keystore. Cloud entries remain until deleted from your account. Moving an item to trash syncs its deleted state; permanently deleting an item removes its cloud copy on the next successful sync. Signing out does not delete local or cloud data. Android backup is disabled for the app's private data.

Security, children, and contact

Cloud data is sent over TLS and protected by account-scoped database rules and provider encryption at rest. No security measure guarantees protection against every possible device, account, or service compromise. Keystore is not directed specifically to children.
